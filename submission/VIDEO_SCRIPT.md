| 54-60 s | Live URL, NASA FIRMS attribution | "A dip is consistent with people scheduling their burning, not proof of cause. Follow the evidence before trusting the trend." |

**Rhythm headline (use this wording):** Sunday dip clear in the US and Australia, weaker in Brazil, and absent where the rest day is Friday or Saturday. Our pre-specified rule passed narrowly; a stricter version fails on Brazil on the 2002-2024 record.

**Numeric anchors (check against the app before recording):** harmonized calendar for 7 countries, 2003-2024. Official rhythm decision pass: US 3/3 streams, Australia 3/3, Brazil 2/3; India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Egypt fail. Brazil's Terra static-source control index 0.913, adjusted p = 0.021.
